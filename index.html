Game log

    Can I target it?

    Hexproof
    Opponents can't target it. Its controller still can.
    Shroud
    Nobody can target it, including its controller.
    Ward
    Opponents can target it, but their spell or ability is countered unless they pay the ward cost.
    Protection from X
    Can't be targeted, blocked, enchanted/equipped, or damaged by anything X. "Destroy all" effects still hit it.
    Indestructible
    Can be targeted. "Destroy" and lethal damage do nothing, but exile, sacrifice and −X/−X still work.
    No word "target"
    Effects that say "each" or "all" ignore hexproof, shroud and ward.

    Commander rules at a glance

    Start
    40 life, 7 cards, one free mulligan. In multiplayer nobody skips their first draw.
    Commander tax
    +{2} for each time you've already cast it from the command zone.
    You lose at
    0 life, 21 combat damage from one commander, 10 poison counters, or drawing from an empty library.
    Command zone
    If your commander dies, is exiled, or would go to your hand or library, you may put it in the command zone instead.
